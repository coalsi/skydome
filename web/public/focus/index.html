The whole picture. Tap a star, or anything far away, to focus on it
  1. At night, press Night: 3 s and tap a bright star in the picture. It's measured on its own. By day, tap something far away, like a rooftop edge.
  2. Turn the lens a little, then stop and wait for the next number: each one comes from a new picture.
  3. Go past the best number until it clearly gets worse, then come back to it.
  4. Press Automatic when you're done, so the camera is back on its own exposure.
